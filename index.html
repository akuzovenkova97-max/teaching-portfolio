How I teach

The principles behind my lessons, with where I've used them.

The Lexical Approach at the heart of it

Everything I design starts from the Lexical Approach. Learners meet language in useful chunks, then put it to work in meaningful communicative practice, and I bring it back through regular revision and meaningful recycling so it sticks.

In practiceThe core of my general English and professional courses, from B1+ lessons to English for IT professionals.

Learning through play

Games, challenges and points turn practice into something learners want to do.

In practiceGame-based modules for children aged 8–12 at EdPalm, where learners help characters complete missions.

Learning through talking

Language grows when people use it with each other, so speaking comes first, not last.

In practiceSpeaking clubs at Educacentre, and Communicative Language Teaching in my courses.

Learning through real tasks

Learners solve problems they'll meet outside the lesson: a meeting, a job interview, a conversation with an international colleague.

In practiceTask-based and scenario-based lessons, and English for IT professionals.

Learning through stories

A story gives new language and ideas a place to live, especially for younger learners.

In practiceEdPalm modules built around two recurring characters, Archimedes and Evrika, and a Halloween series for adults who love horror stories.
